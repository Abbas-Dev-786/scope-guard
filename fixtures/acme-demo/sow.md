# Acme implementation statement of work

## Included scope

- Configure the customer portal and dashboard.
- Implement CSV export for the reporting view.
- Provide a written handover and one remote training session.

## Excluded scope

- Production hosting, infrastructure operations and 24-hour support remain with Acme.
- Paid third-party licenses are not included.

## Commercial baseline

The fixed implementation fee is INR 15000, payable on acceptance of the confirmed baseline.
